Mesh Editor

Boxes are rectangular prisms. Planes are flat, double-sided quads. Both can be any size.
Click a part to select it. With Move or Scale selected, drag a coloured axis handle (red X, green Y, blue Z) to constrain to that axis. Hold Shift while dragging a scale handle for uniform scaling. With Rotate selected, drag the part; hold Shift to lock rotation to the axis of the face you grab. Right-drag orbits; middle-drag pans; the wheel zooms.
Anchored parts can be fitted to the hitbox and never move with joints.
Connecting joins the child at the nearest point between the two surfaces. Floating anchors the child to a point in space; its pivot defaults to the child's center and can be edited below.
Paint tools only appear in this mode. The model is still fully 3D: click any visible face to paint its tile. Right-drag orbits without painting.
Hitbox
The hitbox is centred on X/Z and grows upward from the feet at Y = 0. Anchored parts can be fitted to it exactly.

Texture atlas
Every face owns one 32×32 tile in a shared 1024×1024 atlas. Clicking the model or this flat map paints the matching tile.
Eraser pixels are transparent, so erased areas become holes in the model.
